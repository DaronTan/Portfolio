Project Description

This app was made using HTML, CSS, & JS and was the first app project I had collaborated in with a team.


Boccher is an ecommerce web app that sells musical instruments mainly string instruments. The app design is based on Bocchi the Rock! as there was string instruments played in the anime.


I had used Adobe XD to make the low & high fidelity prototypes for the Boccher mobile & PC website.


I had modelled 2 electric guitars for the project to be used for a 3D feature.


3D Models

For this project, I modelled 2 electric guitars and textured them in Substance 3D Painter. The 2 electric guitars are Gibson Les Paul Custom, & Epiphone Flying V Prophecy.


The 3D Models are low poly and I had learnt trim sheets while texturing the models as there was issues with the trims of the guitars.


The electric guitars are not 1:1 scaled to the actual models, but they have similar designs and research was interesting as I learnt facts about string instruments and music. Below are the Sketchfab controls that you can use to view my models.

Web & UI/UX Design

For this project, I was the lead UI/UX Designer and a web designer, as such I did the prototype low & high fidelity on Adobe XD.


The layout and design of the website is similar to other ecommerce websites and have majority of the features present.


The logo was inspired by Gibson and Epiphone logos with a simple and slick look.


The interface is easy to navigate with a clean look. The website has icons, and texts that are easy to read.
