About

Hello, i am Daron and i am an Immersive Media student currently studying in Ngee Ann Polytechnic.
This website will be my portfolio showcasing some of my works, that I have done throughout my journey learning anything media related.

I aspire to be a game developer or game level designer as creating games are interesting to me. However, I am open to UI/UX, graphic design, 3D Art & other fields of work.

I am currently learning After Effects, a digital visual effects & motion graphics software to create motion graphics, and expanding my knowledge about Maya - a 3D Modelling software & Unity - a Game Engine.

I have picked up HTML, CSS and JavaScript to create websites and develop my front end development skills. Continue scrolling to check out the artworks and games that I have worked on so far!
