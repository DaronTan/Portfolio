Game Project

Hutation
a VR puzzle solving game.

3D VR Unity Project enhanced with Firebase & Meta Quest


Engine

Unity


Softwares

Firebase | Meta Quest | Autodesk Maya | Adobe Substance 3D Painter


Platform

Meta Quest 2 & Quest 3 & Quest Pro


Duration

4 weeks


Team Size

5


Roles

Lead 3D Artist, Lead Game Level designer


Project Description

This game was made in Unity and implemented Meta Quest packages for VR implementation, accompanied by Firebase packages for realtime database features.


The project was a team project incorporating Meta Quest & Firebase to make an engaging VR game experience.


Gameplay

The player is a lab experimented mutant living in space in the future and wishes to escape the lab.


The player has gotten a special ability to magnetise metallic objects to control them through telekinesis.


The game is first person in VR and the player must navigate each room to ultimately escape the ship using the escape pod in the Dome room.


While escaping, the player encounters traps, obstacles set in place to prevent them from leaving. As such, the player must use their abilities to overcome these obstacles.


The types of obstacles are: wall lasers, turrets, platforms, scraps, and scrap deposit bins.


Environment & Level Design

For this project, I was the lead 3D Artist & the lead Game Level Designer. As such, I have made most of the 3D Assets & level layouts.


Since the game was in VR, the graphics of the game could not be as high detailed as I envisoned. But, I have used lighting and modular building to make the levels more vibrant & dynamic.


The game was made with the different levels in mind that have varying levels of gameplay and map size that increases incrementally.


The assets were all made to be optimized for the game & 3D assets in Unity were also tweaked to save on resources.


Hutation Environment Walkthrough video that covers the environments & level maps. Not all gameplay is covered in the walkthrough.


Level 1: The Lab

This level is the first level where the player spawns in.


I decided that the level should be small and easy to navigate in as the player needs to log in to play the game on this level.


I made use of lights & colors to make the map more interesting and vibrant. Lights are baked for game efficiency.


Level 2-4: The maps with increasing gameplay & size

The levels 2-4 are where the main gameplay aspect takes place.


The levels introduce new gameplay mechanics as the player progresses and the map expands in size as well.


I made use of different 3D assets & lighting to make the map more attractive & sets the mood. Lights are baked for game efficiency.


The levels are designed to make the place feel suffocating, uneasy which encourages the player to escape and complete the game.


Level 5: Dome, the final level

Dome is the last level where the player can take the escape pod to complete the game.


The level has no new mechanics but has a different design compared to previous levels.


I made use of lighting, 3D assets & Unity terrain to build the map.


The level is designed to make players feel a sense of freedom, after going through previous levels and they can finally escape the place.
