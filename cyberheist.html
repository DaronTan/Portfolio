Game Project

CYBERHEIST
a First Person Shooter game.

3D Unity Game Project inspired by Payday 2


Engine

Unity


Softwares

Autodesk Maya | Adobe Substance 3D Painter


Platform

Windows PC


Duration

4 weeks


Team Size

3


Roles

Lead 3D Artist, Lead Game Level Designer


Project Description

This game was made in Unity and was the first game project I had collaborated in with a team.


CYBERHEIST is a first-person role-playing shooter that is based on a near future cyberpunk dystopian scenario where you, as the player, can take on heist missions to rob various banks in the district.


There are multiple weapons to choose from which can aid you in the mission. You as the player will be tasked to enter banks, open vaults, loot valuables, all while trying to stay alive and escape as endless hordes of police come after you.


The project was made to explore Unity's features & improve my 3D workflow through experience in modelling game-ready assets.


Gameplay

The player is Robert Robabank, he is a part of an underground criminal organization and an alcoholic.


The player can accept heist contracts from Trifecta, the organization to carry out bank heists. There are different bank maps that players can choose to rob.


The game is a first person shooter and the player can select a gun from a variety of gun choices.


The player can scout the bank and must find the keycard to enter the bank restricted zones to open the vault.


While looting the vault, hordes of police will enter the bank and try to subdue the player. The player needs to loot a minimum amount and escape the bank.


Environment & Level Design

For this project, I was the lead 3D Artist & the lead Game Level Designer. As such, I have made most of the 3D Assets & level layouts.


Since the game was in windows PC, the graphics of the game could be as high detailed as I envisoned. As such, I have used lighting and modular building to make the levels more fitting of the dystopian setting.


The game was made with the different levels in mind that have replayability made in mind.


The assets were all made to be optimized for the game & 3D assets in Unity were also tweaked to save on resources.


CYBERHEIST Environment Showcase video that covers the environments & different bank maps. Not all gameplay is covered in the walkthrough.


Apartment

The apartment is the lobby where the player spawns in. The player can take heist missions in the lobby.


I decided that the lobby is small and made to fit the story of the player, inspired by V's apartment from Cyberpunk 2077.


I made use of lights & colors to make the room more interesting and vibrant. Lights are baked for game efficiency.


The District

The District is where the player can travel to the banks to carry out their heist.


This level is used for travelling and showcasing the 3D environmental assets.


I made use of different 3D assets & lighting to make the map fit the mood as I had envisioned.


The level is a map where I had made use of modular assets to show the dystopian theme, with lights baked and VFX.


Bold Old Bank

This is the first bank level where the player can enter and scout before starting the heist.


This bank has AI civilians, and cops roaming inside. The bank has restricted areas where the player can enter at their own risk.


I made use of different 3D assets & lighting to make the map more attractive & sets the mood. Lights are baked for game efficiency.


The map size is medium and introduces the gameplay mechanics to the player. The bank design is inspired by Payday 2: First World Bank.


New World Bank

This is the second bank level where the player can enter and scout for the heist.


The bank has AI civilians, and cops roaming inside. The restricted area is the vault.


I made use of different 3D assets & lighting to make the map more attractive & sets the mood. Lights are baked for game efficiency.


The map size is small and introduces the gameplay mechanics to the player. The bank has a futuristic cyberpunk theme.
