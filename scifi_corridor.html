Project Description

This game was made in Unity and implemented Meta Quest packages for VR implementation, accompanied by Firebase packages for realtime database features.


The project was a team project incorporating Meta Quest & Firebase to make an engaging VR game experience.


Gameplay

The player is a lab experimented mutant living in space in the future and wishes to escape the lab.


The player has gotten a special ability to magnetise metallic objects to control them through telekinesis.


The game is first person in VR and the player must navigate each room to ultimately escape the ship using the escape pod in the Dome room.


While escaping, the player encounters traps, obstacles set in place to prevent them from leaving. As such, the player must use their abilities to overcome these obstacles.


The types of obstacles are: wall lasers, turrets, platforms, scraps, and scrap deposit bins.


Environment & Level Design

For this project, I was the lead 3D Artist & the lead Game Level Designer. As such, I have made most of the 3D Assets & level layouts.


Since the game was in VR, the graphics of the game could not be as high detailed as I envisoned. But, I have used lighting and modular building to make the levels more vibrant & dynamic.


The game was made with the different levels in mind that have varying levels of gameplay and map size that increases incrementally.


The assets were all made to be optimized for the game & 3D assets in Unity were also tweaked to save on resources.

Modular corridor assets in Unity

The Sci-Fi Corridor has 4 modular assets combined: Corridor Dead End, Corridor Junction Closed, Corridor Straight, Corridor Corner.


All modular corridor assets are modeled by me and the assets are game-ready and are low poly. I have made use of Unity's lighting to fully capture the sci-fi theme with the emissive textures and post processing effects.


Modular Asset - Corridor Dead End

Modular Asset - Corridor Straight

This is the corridor straight asset used to add the walkways of the corridor of the environment to extend the corridor length.

Modular Asset - Corridor Junction

This is the corridor junction asset used to add entrances to rooms of the corridor of the environment for other hero asset rooms.

Modular Asset - Corridor Corner

This is the corridor corner asset used to add a corner to the corridor of the environment to create a sense of depth to the corridor.
